_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of use]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}) between the customer installing **Form Sync – Copy, Version History & Backup for Jira Forms** ("Customer", controller) and **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, IČO 21540951, 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("Vendor", processor), and applies to personal data processed by the App under Article 28 of the GDPR.

1. **Subject matter and duration.** Keeping versions of the Customer's Jira Forms templates, copying, updating and restoring them on the Customer's request, for as long as the App is installed, plus the retention period chosen by the Customer.
2. **Nature and purpose.** Automated reading, storage, comparison and display of Jira Forms templates, and copying, updating, restoring, exporting and importing them, solely to provide the App's functionality.
3. **Types of personal data.** Atlassian account IDs of admins who made changes with the App, and any personal data the Customer's users put into the text of form templates (e.g. names in question labels or default answers).
4. **Data subjects.** The Customer's Jira admins and any persons mentioned in the Customer's form templates.
5. **Instructions.** The Vendor processes personal data only to operate the App as configured by the Customer (projects covered, retention, automatic updates of linked copies) and on documented instructions sent to [t.buchta@post.cz](mailto:t.buchta@post.cz).
6. **Where data is processed.** Exclusively within Atlassian's Forge platform (Forge SQL and Key-Value Store) for the Customer's site, inheriting Atlassian's data residency. The App sends no data outside Atlassian. The Vendor has no access to the stored data.
7. **Sub-processor.** Atlassian (provider of the Forge platform), under Atlassian's own terms and data processing addendum. The Vendor will announce any new sub-processor on this page in advance.
8. **Security.** Data is protected by the security measures of the Atlassian Forge platform (encryption at rest and in transit, tenant isolation). The App re-checks the permissions of the calling user on every request and stores user references as account IDs only.
9. **Confidentiality.** Only the Vendor works on the App and is bound by confidentiality.
10. **Assistance.** The Vendor assists the Customer with data subject requests and data protection obligations as far as the App's design allows (e.g. the Customer can skip projects, shorten retention or uninstall the App). Account IDs of closed Atlassian accounts are erased automatically after the weekly personal data report to Atlassian.
11. **Personal data breaches.** The Vendor notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting the App.
12. **Deletion.** Deleted content is purged after the retention period. On uninstallation, Atlassian deletes the App's data according to the Forge data retention policy.
13. **Audits.** The Vendor makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA on request.
14. **Governing law.** As in the Terms of use.
