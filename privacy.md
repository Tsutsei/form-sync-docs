This policy describes how the Atlassian Marketplace app "Form Sync – Copy, Version History & Backup for Jira Forms" (the "App") processes data. The App is provided by **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, Company ID (IČO) 21540951, registered office 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("we"). Contact: **[t.buchta@post.cz](mailto:t.buchta@post.cz)**.

## 1. What the App stores
Inside your Jira Cloud site's Atlassian Forge storage, the App stores:
- versions of the Jira Forms templates of the projects it covers (questions, settings, conditions, publishing) – not filled-in forms or their answers;
- which copies were made of which form;
- an activity log of changes found, copies, updates, restores and imports, with the Atlassian account ID of the admin who made a change with the App;
- the App's settings.

People are referenced only by their **Atlassian account ID**. The App does not store names, e-mail addresses or avatars; they are looked up from Jira when displayed.

## 2. Where the data is stored
All data is stored in Atlassian's infrastructure (Forge SQL and Forge Key-Value Store) and inherits Atlassian's data residency. The App does not send data to any server outside Atlassian, and we (the vendor) have no access to it. Exports are shown only to the admin who requests them.

## 3. Purpose and legal basis
The data is processed solely to provide the App's function on behalf of the Jira site, which acts as the controller; we act as processor (Art. 28 GDPR).

## 4. Retention
Versions are deleted after the period configured by the site admin (90 days to 5 years), always keeping the newest versions of each form (5 to 100); the activity log after 90 to 730 days. When the App is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the App's data according to its Forge data retention policy.

## 5. Personal data reporting and erasure
The App reports the account IDs it stores to Atlassian weekly via the Personal Data Reporting API and erases every reference to accounts that have been closed.

## 6. Data Processing Addendum
The [Data Processing Addendum]({{ '/dpa/' | relative_url }}) (Art. 28 GDPR) applies to all customers.

## 7. Sharing
We do not sell or share data. Sub-processor: Atlassian (hosting of the Forge platform).

## 8. Your rights
Site admins can skip projects, change retention and uninstall the App at any time. For requests concerning personal data, contact your Jira site admin or us at [t.buchta@post.cz](mailto:t.buchta@post.cz).
